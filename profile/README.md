## About Cubasis

Cubasis is a mobile music production studio built for recording, arranging, and mixing on a touchscreen. Cubasis mobile recording lets songwriters capture ideas the moment they arrive, while Cubasis audio editing and Cubasis MIDI sequencing cover the detailed work that follows. The environment stays close to a traditional studio layout, so tracking, comping, and balancing feel familiar even on a tablet.

Cubasis keeps a full session in one place. Audio tracks, MIDI parts, virtual instruments, and mixer channels share a single project view, which makes Cubasis music production practical for demos, sketches, and finished arrangements. Cubasis mixer routing handles buses, sends, and insert chains without forcing a switch to another tool. Cubasis mobile recording sessions can start small and grow into complete productions as ideas develop.
